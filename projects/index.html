Tridix @ RES Synesis

A 2D and 3D configurator, designed for companies that want to provide their customers with a preview of their product, with the option to customise various details such as colour, material, shape, lighting, scenery, and more.

I mainly worked on the standalone version of the 3D configurator, based on JavaScript and using the Three.js library. The 3D demo shown in the video is available here.
